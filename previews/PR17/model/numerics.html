Numerics ​

Grids ​

The horizontal grid is an Oceananigans RectilinearGrid with a Flat vertical direction, on CPUs or GPUs. Fields use the Arakawa C-grid. The thickness, bed and other scalars sit at cell centres, on x-faces and on y-faces. Shear stresses sit at cell corners.

Column quantities live on the SigmaGrid, a three-dimensional grid with the same horizontal coordinates and Nσ levels in the normalized height above the bed,    . The physical height is   . bed_refinement > 1 clusters the levels toward the bed, where the vertical shear and the temperate layer sit.

Velocity solve ​

The depth-integrated stress balance is nonlinear in the velocity through the viscosity and the friction law. A StressBalanceSolver combines two iterations.

Picard iteration (PicardIteration): freeze the viscosity and drag at the current velocity, solve the linear problem, repeat. The iteration is controlled by

  • Convergence(; reltol, abstol, maxiter): iterate until the residual falls below  ;

  • FixedIterations(N): iterate exactly N times. Within a time-dependent run, a few iterations per step lag the nonlinearity by one step, which is cheap and usually sufficient.

Conjugate gradients (VelocityConjugateGradientSolver) solve each linear problem. Each row of the operator is integrated over its velocity cell, so the operator is symmetric positive definite on any RectilinearGrid. The available preconditioners are

  • JacobiPreconditioner: a stored diagonal;

  • StressBalanceDiagonalPreconditioner (the default): the operator diagonal evaluated from the current coefficients, so it follows the Picard iterations without storage;

  • StressBalanceMultigridPreconditioner: a geometric multigrid V-cycle. Each level halves the grid in x, y or both and rediscretizes the operator. The smoother is ℓ1-Jacobi on points (PointJacobi, the default) or on lines in y (LineJacobi), so the V-cycle stays symmetric positive definite. On flowlines and other grids much longer in x than in y, LineJacobi() needs far fewer iterations.

On a MISMIP+ ice stream with a grounding line and a calving front (benchmarks/multigrid_scaling.jl, one SSA solve from zero to a relative tolerance of ), the diagonal preconditioner needs twice the iterations each time the grid is refined, while multigrid stays nearly flat:

The schedule of the solver (default IterationInterval(1)) can skip velocity solves between time steps.

Thickness transport and time stepping ​

The thickness is advanced with a split Runge–Kutta scheme (timestepper = :SplitRungeKutta3 by default). Each stage clips at zero. The default advection is fifth-order WENO with bounds    km, which needs a horizontal halo of at least 4; UpwindBiased(order = 1) works with any halo. The finite upper bound keeps the limiter's reverse-mode derivative finite.

Two functions give stable time-step bounds:

  • Nunatak.cell_advection_timescale(model): the shortest time for the depth-averaged flow to cross a cell, for an advective CFL condition;

  • Nunatak.cell_diffusion_timescale(model): the explicit diffusion limit of the shallow-ice flux (infinite for the depth-integrated balances); see Stress balances.
