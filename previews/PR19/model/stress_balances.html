Stress balances ​

Three approximations of the Stokes equations are available. The depth-integrated ones (SSA and DIVA) share a single velocity operator and solver; the shallow-ice approximation needs no linear solve.

The default thickness advection with ShallowIce() is Centered(order = 2), so the thickness flux is the diffusive flux of Mahaffy (1976). Explicit time stepping of this flux is stable only for well below the diffusive limit; Nunatak.cell_diffusion_timescale(model) returns the per-direction bound . In two dimensions the linearized shallow-ice diffusivity exceeds and both directions add up, so use a diffusive_cfl well below 1/2 (the Halfar benchmark uses 1/4).

Boundary conditions ​

  • Side walls. Velocities normal to Bounded walls vanish. The tangential condition is side_walls = FreeSlipWalls() (no shear stress at wall corners) or NoSlipWalls() (zero tangential velocity at the wall, imposed with a mirror ghost) in DepthIntegrated(; side_walls).

  • Calving fronts. At a face with ice on one side only, the driving stress is replaced by the net front force    of the ice cell, with its depth below sea level, so the front feels the ocean pressure.

  • Ice-free cells add no stiffness. Velocity points with ice-free cells on both sides are removed from the solve like walls, so the operator stays symmetric as the ice extent changes.
