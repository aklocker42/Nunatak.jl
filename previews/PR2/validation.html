Validation ​

The scripts in benchmarks/ compare Nunatak with analytic solutions and published model results, in cases too slow for the test suite. Run them from that directory with its environment, for example julia --project=. -e 'include("halfar.jl"); halfar()'. The examples are tutorials and make no such comparisons.

ScriptCaseReference
halfar.jlIsothermal similarity dome, 25 kyrHalfar (1983); Bueler et al. (2005), Test B
eismint2_a.jlThermomechanical ice cap grown from zero ice, 200 kyrPayne et al. (2000), experiment A
halfpipe_streamice.jlFloating shelf in a channel with an advancing calving front, 2 yearsMITgcm STREAMICE verification/halfpipe_streamice
halfpipe_streamice_adjoint.jlGradient of a velocity and thickness cost with respect to basal meltMITgcm STREAMICE verification/halfpipe_streamice (input_ad)
mismip_plus.jlMarine ice stream on a retrograde bed: spin-up, Ice1r, Ice1ra, Ice2r, Ice2raAsay-Davis et al. (2016); STREAMICE in Cornford et al. (2020)
kleiner2015_a.jlSlab under a surface temperature cycle, basal melt and refreezingKleiner et al. (2015), experiment A
kleiner2015_b.jlSteady polythermal slab, cold-temperate transition surfaceKleiner et al. (2015), experiment B

Halfpipe against STREAMICE ​

A mostly floating shelf in a half-pipe channel, fed through the west edge, with no-slip side walls and a calving front that advances through partially filled cells. Nunatak against STREAMICE run with converged Picard iterations:

On the same geometry, the maximum speeds of the two models agree to 0.4%. STREAMICE's speed alternates between high and low values from one step to the next, so per-step speeds are not compared.
