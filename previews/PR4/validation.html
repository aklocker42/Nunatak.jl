Validation ​

The scripts in benchmarks/ compare Nunatak with analytic solutions and published model results, in cases too slow for the test suite. Run them from that directory with its environment, for example julia --project=. -e 'include("halfar.jl"); halfar()'. The examples are tutorials and make no such comparisons.

On the same geometry, the maximum speeds of the two models agree to 0.4%. STREAMICE's speed alternates between high and low values from one step to the next, so per-step speeds are not compared.

Square ice shelf on triangles against DJUICE ​

The ISSM/DJUICE test101 case: a 1000 km square floating shelf, isothermal Glen's law with    Pa s, no friction and zero velocity on the whole boundary. Nunatak runs on the exact meshes of DJUICE, read from benchmarks/square_shelf_djuice_reference/, and is compared with DJUICE velocities converged to a Picard tolerance of . The remaining difference comes from the strain-rate regularisation of the viscosity, which DJUICE does not use: with strain_rate_regularization = 1e-16 instead of the default s the error drops to 7.4e-9 on the 50 km mesh and 1.4e-8 on the 20 km mesh.
