Ice masks ​

The ice mask scales the depth-integrated viscosity, so cells with   are ice free and drop out of the stress balance.

SmoothIceMask (the default) rises from 0 at minimum_thickness to 1 at minimum_thickness + transition_thickness as a cubic smoothstep, so it is continuously differentiable in . The ice margin moves wherever the mass balance takes it.

PartialCellFront tracks a calving front with partially filled cells, after Albrecht et al. (2011) and as in MITgcm STREAMICE:

  • is a persistent 0/1 flag of full cells, and only full cells enter the stress balance.

  • A cell that is not full but holds ice is a partial front cell. Ice of reference thickness covers the fraction of it. It receives thickness from full neighbours flowing into it and passes none on.

  • At the end of each step becomes the mean thickness of the full cells flowing in.

  • A partial cell whose mean thickness reaches becomes full. It passes the excess volume to its empty neighbours, up to passes times per step. passes = 0 holds the front fixed.

  • The transport conserves volume. Surface mass balance and melt act on full cells during the step, and then on the ice of partial cells, which empty when the melt removes all of it.

Calving ​

Nunatak has no calving law. A fixed calving front can be imposed in two ways. One is a PartialCellFront with passes = 0. The other is a large melt rate in the ocean beyond the front, which removes any ice that flows there; the MISMIP+ benchmark does this.
