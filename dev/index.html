Nunatak.jl

Nunatak is a differentiable ice-sheet and ice-shelf model built on Oceananigans.jl. It runs on CPUs and GPUs, and Enzyme computes gradients through whole simulations for inversions and sensitivity studies.

Features

  • Stress balances: shallow ice (SIA), the shallow-shelf approximation, and depth-integrated viscosity (DIVA, DIVAShear()), on a staggered C-grid with no-slip or free-slip side walls.
  • Velocity solver: Picard iterations around a preconditioned conjugate-gradient solve, with Jacobi, diagonal or geometric-multigrid preconditioners.
  • Rheology and sliding: Glen's flow law with a constant or Arrhenius rate factor, and Weertman or regularized Coulomb friction.
  • Grounding lines and calving fronts: subgrid grounded fractions, calving-front stresses, and a front that advances through partially filled cells.
  • Thermodynamics: enthalpy with cold and temperate ice, basal water and strain heating, on a terrain-following σ-grid.
  • Adjoints: Enzyme reverse-mode rules for the velocity solve, including a fixed-point adjoint of the Picard iteration, plus checkpointed gradients through time stepping.

Installation

Nunatak is not registered yet. Add it from its repository:

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/aklocker42/Nunatak.jl")

Example

A Halfar (1983) dome evolving under the shallow-ice approximation:

using Oceananigans, Nunatak

spy = 31556926
grid = RectilinearGrid(CPU(); size = (97, 97), halo = (4, 4), x = (-1212.5e3, 1212.5e3), y = (-1212.5e3, 1212.5e3),
                       topology = (Bounded, Bounded, Flat))

model = IceSheetModel(grid; Nσ = 10, stress_balance = ShallowIce(),
                      rheology = GlenFlowLaw(grid; rate_factor = 1e-16 / spy, exponent = 3),
                      friction = WeertmanFriction(grid; coefficient = 1e18, exponent = 1))

set!(model, H = (x, y) -> 3600 * max(0, 1 - (hypot(x, y) / 750e3)^(4 / 3))^(3 / 7))

for _ in 1:100
    time_step!(model, 1spy)
end

examples/ has a friction inversion with Enzyme gradients and two polythermal slab cases.
