Validation ​

The scripts in benchmarks/ compare Nunatak with analytic solutions and published model results, in cases too slow for the test suite. Run them from that directory with its environment, for example julia --project=. -e 'include("halfar.jl"); halfar()'. The examples are tutorials and make no such comparisons.

Grounding line on a flowline against Schoof (2007) ​

A marine flowline on a prograde bed,      m, with Weertman friction ( ,   Pa m⁻¹ᐟ³ a¹ᐟ³),    Pa⁻³ a⁻¹, accumulation 0.3 m a⁻¹ and no buttressing. The boundary-layer theory of Schoof (2007) puts the steady grounding line at 408.0 km. Nunatak runs to steady state from thin ice (advance) and from thick ice grounded past that position (retreat); with a continuum model both would end at the same place, and the gap between them is the resolution error.

Splitting the driving stress at the grounding line (SubgridGroundedFraction(driving_stress = true)) moves the retreated position about 8 km closer at both resolutions and leaves the advance unchanged. Refining only the band the grounding line crosses costs about half as much as refining the whole flowline.

Grounded ice does not calve under FrontVelocityCalving, as the protocol asks. In Exp4 the front advances freely after 500 a, and the maximum extent stops it at its starting position.

The CalvingMIP outputs follow from the partially filled front cells. A cell with ice mask   and thickness below its front thickness covers the fraction of its area with ice thick. A cell counts as ice when it is at least half covered, and as grounded when its grounded fraction is at least one half. This gives the mask (1 grounded, 2 floating, 3 ocean) and lithk. The areas weight each cell by its coverage. Along each profile, the front is where the bilinearly interpolated coverage falls through one half.

The front displacement is the change in distance from the centre of the front on each profile, in the direction the prescribed front velocity acts. The Caprona profiles end about 742 km from the centre, inside the initial front, so their displacement is measured from 750 km once the front crosses them. The grounded area stays at 921 × 10³ km² and the mass above flotation changes by less than 0.1 %.

In Exp4 the Halbrane fronts retreat 233 km by 481 a against the prescribed 239 km at 500 a. The Caprona fronts retreat over floating ice but lag the prescribed motion by 15 to 20 %, reaching 189 km at 500 a. After 500 a the front advances freely and is back at 750 km on the Halbrane profiles at 860 a, held there by the maximum extent. The grounded area falls from 937 to 892 × 10³ km² at 500 a and recovers to 918 × 10³ km².
