Halfpipe against STREAMICE

A mostly floating shelf in a half-pipe channel, fed through the west edge, with no-slip side walls and a calving front that advances through partially filled cells. Nunatak against STREAMICE run with converged Picard iterations:

On the same geometry, the maximum speeds of the two models agree to 0.4%. STREAMICE's speed alternates between high and low values from one step to the next, so per-step speeds are not compared.
